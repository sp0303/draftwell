FAQ

Questions about Resumely AI

What is Resumely AI?

Resumely AI is a free AI resume builder. Upload the resume you already have (PDF, Word or text) and the AI turns it into an editable resume. Pick one of eight ATS-friendly themes, tailor it to a job description, edit it on the page or by chatting with the AI assistant, and download a PDF.

Is Resumely AI free?

Yes. Resumely AI is a free personal project, and every account starts with free AI credits. The AI runs on free models with daily limits, so at busy times it can be slower or switch to simpler built-in rules for a while.

How does the ATS resume checker work?

Paste a job description and Resumely AI reads the job's keywords, then scores your resume out of 100: job keywords (40), relevant experience (20), job title (15), readable format (15) and specific results (10). It shows which keywords your resume has and which are missing. The score is a guide to how keyword-matching systems read a resume, not a promise from any employer.

Can the AI tailor my resume to a job description?

Yes. The AI rewords what you have already done in the job's own language and moves your most relevant work to the top. It never adds skills, jobs or numbers you don't have: requirements your resume doesn't cover are listed so you can decide.

Are the resume templates ATS-friendly?

Yes. All eight themes fit on one page, use standard section headings and real text rather than images, so applicant tracking systems can read them. The downloaded PDF matches the preview exactly.

Does it check spelling and grammar?

Yes. The AI reads every line and lists typos, grammar slips and inconsistencies such as mixed tenses, each with a one-click fix. Names of people, companies and technologies are left alone.

Which files can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx) or plain text, up to 10 MB. Scanned images can't be read, so use a text-based PDF or a Word file.

Is my resume private?

You sign in with Google, and only you can see your resumes. You can delete a resume at any time. When you use an AI feature, the resume text is sent to the AI provider that answers the request (OpenRouter or NVIDIA), and free AI providers may keep logs of what they receive.
