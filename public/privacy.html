Who we are

Resumely AI is a free AI resume builder at resume.sarey.tech. It is a personal project by Sharat Patnayakuni, published under Sarey (sarey.tech). It is not a commercial product.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google

Your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.

When you use the app

  • The resume files you upload (PDF, Word or text) and the text read from them.
  • Your resumes as you edit them, with the theme and color you choose.
  • Your messages to the AI assistant and its replies.
  • Job descriptions you paste, the keywords read from them, and ATS scores.

Usage

  • Your AI credit balance and counts of uploads, PDF downloads and AI requests.
  • A record of each AI request: which feature, which AI model and provider, how long it took and how many tokens it used. These records are deleted after 30 days.

Cookies and browser storage

  • One cookie, rl_sid, keeps you signed in. It can't be read by scripts on the page and expires after 180 days without use. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Your browser remembers which resume you last opened and whether keyword highlighting is on. This stays on your device.

Technical logs

Like any website, our server and network keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, browser type and errors, to keep the site secure and fix problems.

How we use it

  • To run Resumely AI: store your resumes, show them only to you, read uploads, tailor resumes, score them and make PDFs.
  • To manage free AI credits and usage limits.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your resumes to train AI models of our own.

AI providers

The AI features (reading an upload, chatting with the assistant, tailoring to a job, reading a job's keywords and checking spelling and grammar) send the text they need, such as your resume content, a job description or your message, to one of these services:

  • OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), which passes the request to the provider of the AI model it uses, such as Baidu (ERNIE), Particle or NVIDIA (Nemotron).
  • NVIDIA API catalog (build.nvidia.com).

These are free AI services, and the providers behind free models may keep logs of what they receive and may use it under their own terms. If there is something you'd rather not share, such as your phone number or home address, remove it from your resume before using the AI features. Editing on the page, choosing themes and downloading PDFs work without AI.

Other services

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic to the site, protects it from attacks, and provides privacy-friendly visit statistics that don't use cookies.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when pages load.
  • Your data is stored in a database and on a cloud server that we run.

Who can see it

Your resumes are only shown to you when you're signed in. The admin page shows each account's name, email, credit balance and usage counts, but not resume contents. As the person running the service, Sarey has technical access to the database and server for maintenance and only uses it for that.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and resumes are kept until you delete them.
  • Deleting a resume removes it and its uploaded file.
  • Deleting your account removes your account, every resume, every uploaded file and your sign-in sessions. AI request records lose the link to you and are deleted within 30 days.
  • Technical logs are kept for a limited time and then removed.

Your choices

  • See and edit everything you've written, download your resume as a PDF and download your original upload.
  • Delete any resume from Your resumes on the upload page.
  • Delete your whole account from Delete my account at the bottom of the upload page.
  • Sign out at any time, which ends the session on that browser.
  • Email us for a copy of your data or any other privacy request.

Security

Connections from your browser use HTTPS, the sign-in cookie can't be read by page scripts, and every request checks that a resume belongs to you before showing or changing it. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put information in your resume that you'd be worried to have exposed.

Children

Resumely AI is not meant for children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
